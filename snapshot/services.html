Why It Matters for Your Business

Search is no longer just ten blue links. Google now answers many questions directly with AI Overviews, and millions of buyers ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity which product, clinic, agency or course to choose. When an AI answer appears, far fewer people scroll down and click, so ranking alone is no longer enough. You need to be the brand the AI quotes.

AI SEO combines strong classic SEO with the signals AI systems rely on. I make sure search engines and language models can crawl your site, understand exactly who you are and what you offer, and find clear, trustworthy answers they can cite. That means clean technical foundations, structured data, well organised topic clusters and content that answers real questions in plain language.

The upside is significant. Visitors who arrive from AI search have usually done their research already, which is why they convert at a much higher rate than traditional organic visitors. Every month you appear in those answers, you build authority that competitors find hard to catch.

What You Get

  • Technical SEO. Fix crawl errors, indexing gaps, site structure, Core Web Vitals and mobile issues that hold rankings back.

Why It Matters for Your Business

Google Ads captures people at the exact moment they are searching for what you sell. That intent is why search ads convert at such strong rates across industries. It is also why wasted spend hurts: every irrelevant search term or broken conversion tag takes budget away from real buyers.

I build Google Ads accounts on clean conversion tracking first, using GA4, Google Tag Manager and enhanced conversions so Smart Bidding learns from real leads and sales. Search campaigns are organised into tight themes with strong ad copy, and Performance Max, Shopping and YouTube are added where they fit your goals and budget.

After launch, the work is continuous. I review search terms, add negative keywords, adjust bids and budgets, and test ads and landing pages every week. FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions .

What digital marketing services do you offer?

I offer six connected services: AI SEO, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Ads, Google Ads, website and app development, marketing automation and lead generation. You can hire me for one service or combine several into a full growth system.

What is AI SEO and how is it different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking pages in Google's blue links. AI SEO also optimizes your content, entities and schema so AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews understand your brand and cite it in their answers. It builds on strong technical SEO rather than replacing it.

Should I choose Meta Ads or Google Ads?

Google Ads works best when people already search for what you sell, while Meta Ads is stronger for creating demand and reaching people by interest and behavior. Many businesses get the best results using both together. My guide Facebook Ads vs Google Ads explains how to choose.

How long does it take to see results?

Paid campaigns on Meta and Google usually start producing data and leads within the first few weeks, then improve as testing continues. SEO and AI SEO build over time, and most websites see meaningful organic growth within three to six months depending on competition and starting point.

Do you work with businesses outside Bangladesh?

Yes. I work remotely with local businesses, international brands, startups and personal brands. Strategy calls, reporting and day-to-day communication all happen online, mostly over WhatsApp, email and video calls.
